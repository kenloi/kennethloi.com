About.

Southern California kid raised on science fiction and beef noodle soup.

Currently, I Study RNA-guided systems in the Doudna lab.

I'm drawn to the strange moments when biology invents new primitives: genetic parasites becoming CRISPR, endosymbionts becoming organelles, retroviruses becoming placentas. Evolution has a habit of turning weirdness into infrastructure.

I look for that pattern everywhere. Science creates the 0-to-1s; the world scales them into 1-to-1000s. Progress is rate-limited by the former.

I want to spend my life working on both. If you're working on the zeroes, let's chat.
